Resolve Telecom Customer Support Knowledge Base

1. ROUTER BLINKING OR SOLID RED LIGHT:
- A blinking red light on the FiberBox X1 indicates loss of optical signal (LOS) from the street line.
- Step 1: Check that the green fiber optic cable is pushed firmly into the PON port until it clicks.
- Step 2: Unplug the power adapter for 30 seconds and plug it back in.
- Step 3: If the light remains red after 5 minutes, a field technician visit is required.

2. SLOW WI-FI SPEEDS:
- Ensure devices are connected to the 5GHz band (SSID ending in _5G) rather than 2.4GHz.
- Keep the router away from microwaves, thick concrete walls, and cordless phone bases.
- Run a wired speed test via Ethernet to verify line speed before troubleshooting wireless interference.

3. BILLING AND REFUND POLICY:
- Service outages lasting longer than 4 consecutive hours qualify for a pro-rated bill credit.
- Hardware returns must be initiated within 30 days of cancellation using the prepaid shipping label.
- Unreturned FiberBox X1 routers incur a $150 equipment fee after 30 days.

4. ACCOUNT AND PLAN UPGRADES:
- Plan upgrades take effect within 15 minutes with no technician visit required.
- Static IP addresses are available on Business Fiber tiers for an additional $15/month.
